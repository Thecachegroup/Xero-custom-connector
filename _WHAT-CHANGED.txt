1. THE REPEATING TEMPLATE AUDIT - the reason to deploy today

fill_period_drafts only fills a line whose quantity is currently ZERO. That
guard is what makes four sweeps across a billing week safe: a filled line is
never refilled.

The corollary was never checked. A template that generates a NON-ZERO quantity
is invisible to the fill. Nothing corrects it, and it invoices the client that
quantity every period until somebody reads the number.

Bhasker Veela's sales template generates at 1. His August invoice reached
Awaiting Approval at ONE day, $320, against 21 days worked - about $6,573
under-billed, one approval from going out.

    list_repeating_templates()

marks every line that generates non-zero with <<<. Fix each one:

    set_repeating_quantity('Linfox - BV', 0)        dry by default

The template is read back from Xero and re-posted with its own values, so
nothing but the quantity moves.

You do not yet know how many others are like this. That is the point.

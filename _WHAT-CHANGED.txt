0. THE AUDIT WAS FLAGGING 80 LINES OF WHICH ONE WAS WRONG

Found by running it against the live org on 3 September. list_repeating
_templates() returned 364 lines and marked 80 <<<. Working through them:

   62   DELETED templates. They generate nothing at all.
   18   fixed-fee lines - the Xenon Media monthly billing, the office
        cleaning, and Deepti and Vivek, who are on an annual fee split over
        twelve and billed as ONE UNIT of a monthly amount. Quantity 1 is
        CORRECT on all of those. Zeroing Deepti's would have stopped a
        $9,583.33 monthly invoice.
    1   actually wrong: Bhasker.

A report where nine flags in ten are noise catches nothing - the same failure
the coverage report had when it came back with six SEEK ads in it. So:

  - DELETED templates hidden. include_deleted=True to see them.
  - fixed-fee lines listed separately under "NOT a fault, do not zero these",
    summarised by contact. include_fixed_fee=True for the full table.
  - the flag itself is now ASYMMETRY, not just non-zero. A line is day-rate
    when the SAME item code has another LIVE template sitting at zero - the
    matching bill, or the matching sale. One of the pair was reset and the
    other was missed. That is exactly Bhasker: VVR bill at 0, Linfox sale at
    1. Deepti and Vivek have both sides at 1 and no zero anywhere, which is
    what a fixed fee looks like.

Live output is now one row. It was eighty.

CONFIRM ONE THING: that Deepti (Linfox - DBL) and Vivek (Linfox - VKC) really
are fixed monthly fees rather than days x a rate. The arithmetic says so -
$9,583.33 is $115,000/12 and $8,333.33 is $100,000/12 - but it is your call,

0b. THE FILL WAS STAMPING A FORTNIGHT REFERENCE ON MONTHLY PEOPLE

Found while checking Prasanthi. reference="auto" builds a FORTNIGHT range -
"17 August to 30 August 2026" - and it was applied to every sales draft
carrying an item code in the quantities list, monthly people included.

Two things wrong with that. Prasanthi's month runs the 12TH TO THE 11TH, not
the calendar month, so a fortnight range on her invoice is wrong in length AND
in dates. And the house reference for a monthly person is a MONTH LABEL, not a
range - "Deepti Bansal May 2026" is the real format.

Monthly people are now excluded from the auto reference and their CURRENT
reference is reported instead, under MONTHLY - REFERENCE NOT TOUCHED, so a
broken one is visible rather than silently overwritten. Her live draft
TCG-21207 currently reads "Prasanthi Dharanikota  August Year]" - a merge field
that did not resolve in the Xero template. That needs fixing in Xero; nothing
here will now paper over it.

The days are still filled. Only the reference is withheld.

Cadence is read from config/roster_overrides.json, which is where it already
lives - no network, no second table. NOTE: roster.load_overrides() returns the
by_item_code map, not the whole file. Getting that wrong made the guard return
False for everybody and do nothing at all; there is now a test pinning it.

NEW FIELD - period_day, and it is now WRITTEN, not just recorded.

Prasanthi is 12: the 12th of the preceding month to the 11th of the current
one. Your words, 3 September - "the twelfth of August to the eleventh of
September would be what you put in her reference." So fill_period_drafts now
generates exactly that:

    Linfox - JJ   fortnightly   "17 August to 30 August 2026"
    Linfox - PD   offset month  "12 August to 11 September 2026"
    Linfox - BV   calendar month - left alone, reported

Same formatter as the fortnightly reference, so the invoices read
consistently, and the year-end case ("12 December 2026 to 11 January 2027")
comes free. The cycle chosen is the one CONTAINING period_end, matching how
the fortnightly reference describes the period being billed rather than the
one before it - the 11th belongs to the cycle that opened on the 12th of the
month before, and there is a test for that boundary.

Absent period_day means calendar month, and NO reference is written for those:
Bhasker, Deepti and Vivek keep whatever their template generated and it is
reported so you can see it. Their house format is a month label - "Deepti
Bansal May 2026" - not a range, and the connector is not going to invent one.

Prasanthi is the only offset person as at 3 September 2026. Confirmed by you.

1. THE FILL NOW SAYS WHEN THE NUMBER ON THE LINE IS NOT THE NUMBER YOU GAVE

This is the important one.

fill_period_drafts never overwrites a line that already carries a quantity -
that rule is what makes four sweeps in a billing week safe, and it has not
changed. But it reported every such line as:

    "already billed - left alone"

Bhasker's line carried 1 because his repeating template generated at 1, not
because anybody billed it. You had passed "Linfox - BV: 21" in the very same
call. Both numbers were in hand and nothing compared them, so ~$6,573
under-billed was reported as reassurance.

plan_line_fill now compares them:

    same as you asked      "already at 21 - left alone"        quiet
    different              "MISMATCH - line has 1, you said 21"

and fill_period_drafts prints mismatches in their own block, above everything
else, headed *** QUANTITY MISMATCH - READ THIS ***, with the fix. Benign repeat
sweeps stay quiet underneath - a warning that fires every fortnight is a warning
nobody reads.

Still never overwritten. A line somebody set deliberately is theirs.

2. SET_REPEATING_QUANTITY NOW PASSES THE WRITE GATE

It did not. Every other write in the connector routes through writes.py and
hits _guard() - TCG_WRITE_ENABLED. This one called xero_client.post_repeating
_invoice() directly and went straight out. The single most consequential write
in the codebase, the one that changes what a client is billed every period with
nobody watching, was the only one TCG_WRITE_ENABLED=false did not stop.

New writes.update_repeating_template() is guarded like everything else.
xero_client.post_repeating_invoice() now raises NotImplementedError pointing at
it, so the bypass cannot be used again by accident.

3. THE DERIVED MONEY FIELDS ARE NO LONGER ECHOED BACK

It posted the raw object read from Xero, so a template went back carrying
Quantity 0 beside LineAmount 320, plus SubTotal / TotalTax / Total from before
the change. That asks Xero to reconcile two answers about the same line and
which one wins is not something to discover on a live client template.

writes.py already solved this for invoices - _LINE_KEEP drops LineAmount, with a
comment explaining why. The lesson had not been carried across to templates. It
has now: LineAmount and TaxAmount off the line, SubTotal/TotalTax/Total off the
document, everything Xero needs to keep the template identical retained,
including LineItemID (without it Xero adds a second line beside the first).

4. IT REFUSES TO WRITE WHAT IT SHOULD NOT, AND PROVES WHAT IT DID

  - a DELETED template is skipped, not posted to
  - a template with no RepeatingInvoiceID is refused: Xero would create a
    SECOND template rather than update the one you meant, and then two
    templates invoice the same client every month
  - after a real write, every template it touched is READ BACK from Xero and
    the quantity confirmed, printed under READ BACK FROM XERO. It used to end
    with "run list_repeating_templates() to confirm" - which asks somebody to go
    and read a number, on the one tool that exists because nobody reads numbers
  - a failure on one template no longer stops the rest; it is reported under
    FAILED with Xero's own validation message

5. DEPENDENCIES PINNED

    requests<3  pandas<3  openpyxl<4  tabulate<0.10  python-dotenv<2

mcp had an upper bound already, added after 2.0.0 relocated FastMCP on 28 July
and took the server down for a day. The other five were unbounded and could do
exactly the same thing on any deploy. This was open item 3 in the state doc.

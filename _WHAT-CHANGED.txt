XERO CONNECTOR — TAX BASIS ON BILLS, AND BILL NUMBERS ON THEIR OWN
Built 4 September 2026 Melbourne, off github.com/Thecachegroup/Xero-custom-connector main (17ab1bd).

214 tests before, 230 after. All pass. The 16 new ones were run against the
UNFIXED code first and 14 failed. The 2 that passed assert behaviour that
already existed and must not change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
list_repeating_templates flagged six BILL templates as TAX INCLUSIVE against
the house rule: That's Sparkling Clean, DITYA, Logic Lanes, PRAVID, VVR and
Karen Crabb. Every one of the six is correct as it stands.

Five carry no GST at all — the offshore monthlies post BASEXCLUDED and Karen
Crabb's NZ bill has no Australian GST. On a zero-rated line Inclusive and
Exclusive are the same number, so the basis cannot do any harm.

The sixth, That's Sparkling Clean, does carry 10% GST and is Inclusive on
purpose: the $132 IS the GST-inclusive total on their invoice.

And it was not just noise. plan_submission HELD every Inclusive document out
of Awaiting Approval, so all six bills would be refused every run and the only
way through was to set them Exclusive — wrong for all six. A guard that blocks
correct work is a bug.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
BILLS ONLY — read this bit
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Neither carve-out touches a TCG SALES INVOICE. A bill is the supplier's
document and they choose the basis. A sales invoice is ours, contracted ex
GST, and Inclusive on one of those short-invoices the client and takes a
credit note to unwind — so it is still reported and still held, whatever the
tax on it happens to be, and whoever the customer is. Four tests cover that.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
src/writes.py
  NEW  carries_gst(doc) -> True / False / None
       TaxAmount across the lines first, then TaxType against a set of
       zero-rated types. Returns None — NOT False — when the document does not
       say enough to tell, so a trimmed payload keeps the strict behaviour
       instead of being waved through as GST-free.
  NEW  _is_bill(doc, label) — Xero's Type leads; where it is absent the
       caller's label decides, and anything unrecognised is NOT a bill.
  NEW  _inclusive_is_deliberate(doc, inclusive_ok) — contact-name match,
       case and padding insensitive.
  MOD  tax_basis_problems(docs, label, inclusive_ok=frozenset())
       On a BILL, skips a document carrying no GST and one whose supplier is
       named. On a sales invoice, nothing changes.
  MOD  plan_submission(..., inclusive_ok=frozenset(), kind="")
       Same two carve-outs, bills only. A real fault — a Linfox day-rate line
       at 10% set Inclusive — is still held, and there is a test for it.
  MOD  plan_number_change now marks a row "ByContact" when it matched on the
       SUPPLIER NAME rather than an item code.

src/server.py
  NEW  _tax_inclusive_ok() — reads config/tax_inclusive_ok.json. A missing or
       unreadable file returns nothing, which RESTORES the strict behaviour
       rather than silently waiving the check.
  MOD  wired into list_repeating_templates, fill_period_drafts and
       submit_period_invoices.
  MOD  fill_period_drafts no longer refuses a call that passes bill_numbers
       with no quantities. That is the Sparkling Clean case: a fixed-fee line
       correctly sitting at quantity 1, needing only their invoice number.
  MOD  A bill matched by SUPPLIER NAME now gets that number in its REFERENCE
       field as well as its number field. Andrew, 4 September 2026: "that's
       how that company works out who's paid and who hasn't." Contractor bills
       matched by item code are untouched.

config/tax_inclusive_ok.json   NEW — That's Sparkling Clean.
tests/test_tax_basis.py        16 new tests.

WHY
An invoice number already billed in an earlier period is not evidence for this
one. For the fortnight ending 30 August 2026 Bilal Virk re-sent INV-0016 and
Jay Jhala re-sent 20260802 - both already billed on the 16 August run. The
sweep filed both without complaint. Their documents were moved to _QUERY by
hand, and then a RE-SWEEP on 3 September wrote fresh copies straight back into
the live folders, because the sweep holds no memory of that move.
attach_period_files then planned to put the wrong fortnight's timesheets onto
TCG-21200 and TCG-21195. Caught in the dry run, one step before the client
would have been sent them.

This fixes it at the point the decision is made: the file never lands in the
live folder, so there is nothing to quarantine and nothing to un-quarantine.

HOW IT WORKS
_prior_invoice_numbers() reads every ACCPAY bill dated before this fortnight's
Monday and builds {item code: {invoice number: period it was billed for}}.
Keyed on ITEM CODE, never a contact name - Mudassir Ali's bills sit under
Datacraft Consulting Services Pty Ltd. VOIDED bills excluded: a voided number
is free to reuse.

plan_filing() checks each attachment that classifies as an invoice. On a hit
the WHOLE MESSAGE is refused, timesheets included - they cover the period that
invoice covers. The person still counts as having sent, so they are not also
listed under NOT SENT ANYTHING.

THE TWO RULES THAT STOP FALSE POSITIVES
1. A number of 5+ characters matches anywhere in the filename. A shorter one
   ("0013", "0025") matches only as a whole token, so it is not found inside a
   date or a longer number.
2. The subject line is consulted ONLY when the attachment filename carries no
   digits at all. A reply on the old INV-0016 thread carrying INV-0017 is
   judged on the attachment, not the subject.

FAILS SOFT
If the Xero lookup throws, the sweep still runs. It loses the guard and says
so under "DUPLICATE CHECK DID NOT RUN", which is how it behaved before this
existed. plan_filing without prior_invoice_numbers behaves exactly as before -
there is a test for that.

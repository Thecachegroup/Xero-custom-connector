WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The cadence register already existed and is the right shape: config/
roster_overrides.json, keyed on item code, one line per exception, no code

PETER SMALL WAS NOT. He defaulted to fortnightly, and on 4 September 2026 the
fortnightly fill was one write away from stamping "17 August to 30 August 2026"

Adding him as plain "monthly" is not the fix. split_excluded judges a document
monthly when ANY line carries a monthly item code, whatever the document is, so

He is billed FORTNIGHTLY by TecAlliance and invoiced MONTHLY to Xenon Media.
One person, two cycles. Andrew, 4 September 2026: "there will always be the
exception."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CHANGE — IT STAYS IN CONFIG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
`cadence` may now be a mapping as well as a string:

    "Tec - PS": {
      "name": "Peter Small",
      "cadence": { "sales": "monthly", "bills": "fortnightly" }
    }

A plain "cadence": "monthly" still means both sides, so the offshore four are
untouched. Only somebody genuinely split needs the longer form. Adding the next
exception is still one entry in one file.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
src/server.py
  NEW  _cadence_value(entry, side) — 'monthly' or 'fortnightly' for one side.
       ONLY 'monthly' is acted on; a typo, a null or an unknown value reads as
       fortnightly, so a malformed entry cannot quietly take somebody out of
       the fortnightly run. side='' means "monthly on either side".
  MOD  _monthly_item_codes(side="") and _cadence_of(item_code, side="").
  MOD  fill_period_drafts asks for the SALES cadence when deciding a sales
       reference — that is the write that nearly went wrong.
  MOD  submit_period_invoices passes {"sales": ..., "bills": ...}.

src/writes.py
  MOD  split_excluded accepts a plain set (both sides, as before) or a mapping
       {"sales", "bills"}. A bill is judged on the bills cadence, a sales
       invoice on the sales one. A document with no Type falls back to the
       SALES set — a sales invoice going out a cycle early is the costlier
       mistake.

config/roster_overrides.json
  NEW  Tec - PS, with the reasoning and the August arithmetic recorded.
  MOD  the header comment documents the split form.

tests/test_cadence.py   11 new tests.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO — still to build
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
This stops a monthly invoice being damaged by the fortnightly run. It does NOT
raise one, and it does not tell you when one is due and unbilled.

TCG-21192 sat at zero for a week and was noticed by eye, not by a tool. The
monthly-due check — for each monthly-sales person, is this month's invoice
raised and still at zero, and what do the fortnightly bills DATED in that month
total — is a separate build and is not in this package. For Peter that
calculation returns 138.14 on its own.

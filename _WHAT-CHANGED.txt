TCG Xero connector - the roster now comes from Xero
Built for Andrew Hurnard, 2 September 2026, Melbourne. Off main.

DO NOT UPLOAD _WHAT-CHANGED.txt. Everything else is the repo.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RUN THIS FIRST AFTER DEPLOYING

    roster_diagnostics()

It is read-only. It prints the roster Xero produces, the addresses it will
match, anyone set up but unbillable, and anyone carrying more than one live
item. Check it against who you expect BEFORE the Friday sweep relies on it.

The roster logic is proven against fixtures built from your real item codes and
addresses - 118 tests, up from 85 - but it has never yet run against live Xero.
roster_diagnostics is how you find out, without a sweep acting on the answer.

WHAT CHANGED

1. THE ROSTER IS DERIVED, NOT LISTED.  new file src/roster.py

   config/contractor_mail.json is no longer read by anything in the sweep. The
   roster is built at run time:

     PAYG   - payroll employees, name resolved to an item code through the same
              map the invoice check has used for months
     ABN    - the contact on a repeating BILL, linked by the item code on its
              line. "D & L Solutions Pty Ltd" says Don Vuong nowhere; the line
              says Linfox - DV, and that is the only reliable link.

   Archived (z-prefixed) and inactive items are dropped. An item with neither a
   payroll record nor a repeating bill is dropped too, which is how
   Passthru - Expenses stays out without being named anywhere.

   A joiner appears the moment they are set up to be paid. A leaver drops off
   when their template stops. Nobody edits a file.

2. SENDERS ARE MATCHED BY NAME.  src/mail_mappers.py

   Order, and it does not change: a known address wins; then the cc and
   reply-to; then a forward from one of our own domains; then the name.

   Nine of ten of your contractor addresses carry the person's own name -
   devinia_liddelow, jachakkshitija, karenmareecrabb, mudassirali27, donvuong,
   jhalajay, richaarora16, mdmazherali, jerry_gonsalves. The tenth,
   techneitconsulting@gmail.com, carries their Xero CONTACT name, which is
   matched too. All nine are in the tests, by address, asserting the right
   person.

   Guard rails, because names are looser than addresses:
     - an address (or contact name in the address) is enough on its own
     - a display name, a subject line or a name in the body is NOT. Each needs
       a second signal agreeing with it
     - a display name alone is never accepted, at any strength
     - two people matching equally well is REPORTED, never resolved. Mudassir
       Ali and Mazher Ali are brothers, one seat, item codes one character
       apart - there is a test for exactly that
     - a forward from one of our own addresses is never attributed by name.
       Your own "FW: Don Vuong timesheet" is not from Don Vuong
     - Reckon, MYOB and the like are flagged "OPEN IT, there is a document
       inside" rather than dropped as junk - and resolve properly when the
       contractor is in the cc, which is how Reckon sends

3. A TIMESHEET TYPED INTO THE EMAIL BODY IS NO LONGER INVISIBLE.

   The sweep's unit of work was an attachment, so a matched contractor who
   attached nothing produced no plan entry and fell through to NOT SENT
   ANYTHING - identical to someone who never wrote. That is how Devinia
   Liddelow was reported missing for the fortnight ending 30/08 when her
   timesheet had been in the mailbox since the Tuesday.

   Those now come back under "body_only": the message is saved whole as .eml
   into her own folder, named like everything else, and she stops counting as
   missing. The sweep prints it loudly:

     TIMESHEET IS IN THE EMAIL BODY (n) - no attachment to file, so the
     message itself is saved as .eml.
     READ IT AND KEY THE DAYS BY HAND - nothing here counts the days

   It cannot count hours out of a typed table and does not pretend to. What it
   can do is stop her disappearing.

   New GraphClient.message_mime() fetches the raw message. No new Graph
   permission - Mail.Read already covers it, so none of this is gated on the
   Exchange access policy you have not run yet.

4. THE COVERAGE GAP IS REPORTED IN THE SWEEP ITSELF.

   SET UP BUT CANNOT BE BILLED - an active inventory item with no payroll
   record and no repeating bill. Mazher Ali worked five days that way in the
   fortnight ending 30/08 and no draft was ever raised. An item is NOT enough;
   the repeating templates are what raise drafts, and nothing checked for that
   before.

   roster_diagnostics also lists people carrying more than one live item -
   yours currently shows Mazher Ali on three (Linfox - MAZ at 1000/1225, plus
   zLinfox - MA and zLinfox - MAZ still Active at an old 900/1103).

5. The UNMATCHED block now prints WHY each message did not match, rather than
   "nobody in config/contractor_mail.json has these addresses".
